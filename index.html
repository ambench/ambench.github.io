Overview

AM-Bench is designed to systematically evaluate aerial manipulation across diverse dimensions: (Left) a range of platforms, including underactuated (UA-Quad, UA-Hexa) to fully/over actuated (FA-Hexa, Omni-Hexa) systems and different low-level controllers; (Center) a diverse suite of manipulation tasks with domain randomization in spatial poses and textures; (Right) various visuomotor policy architectures and realistic physical constraints, including aerodynamic disturbances (ground/wall effects) and actuator saturation. u, φ(·, ·), s, a, π(·), and o refer to control input, controller, state, high-level action, policy, and observation, respectively.

Headline Results

The current paper evaluates each high-level policy over 30 rollouts on each of 12 tasks using FA-Hexa and the EE-target IK–PID interface. These figures are a fixed paper snapshot, not a live leaderboard.
