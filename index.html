AM-Bench: A Modular Simulation Suite and Benchmark for Aerial Manipulation Policy Learning

Project under anonymous review.

Overview

AM-Bench is designed to systematically evaluate aerial manipulation across diverse dimensions: (Left) a range of platforms, including underactuated (UA-Quad, UA-Hexa) to fully/over actuated (FA-Hexa, Omni-Hexa) systems and different low-level controllers; (Center) a diverse suite of manipulation tasks with domain randomization in spatial poses and textures; (Right) various visuomotor policy architectures and realistic physical constraints, including aerodynamic disturbances (ground/wall effects) and actuator saturation. $u$, $\phi(\cdot, \cdot)$, $s$, $a$, $\pi(\cdot)$, $o$ refer to control input, controller, state, high-level action, policy, and observation, respectively.
