## Software foundations

AM-Bench is built as an Isaac Lab extension and uses Isaac Sim for simulation. Its maintained learning paths integrate LeRobot, ACT, Diffusion Policy through the UMI codebase, and OpenPI. The controller stack integrates Pyroki and acados for optional IK and MPC paths.

See [Resources](resources.md) for project and dependency links, and [Paper and Citation](paper.md) for the citation record.
